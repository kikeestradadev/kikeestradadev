01

BetAnySports Platform Modernization

BetAnything.eu · high-traffic sportsbook

Senior Web Developer Lead · BetUS / BetAnything · 2021–2025

Problem

A multi-brand sportsbook needed platform modernization and a planned 12-month rebrand without disrupting high-traffic operations.

What I built

Owned product architecture for BetAnything.eu, unblocked complex property delivery the team had been unable to ship, and established testing standards across the release cycle.

Architecture

React and Remix on the frontend, Strapi as a headless CMS, with WordPress, PHP, and Laravel still in the multi-brand estate. Testing with Jest and Cypress became part of delivery, not an afterthought.

Decision

Modernize behind a stable public product: reusable architecture and release confidence first, then the rebrand — instead of a disruptive rewrite.

Result

The 12-month rebrand and migration shipped in 6 months. The platform supports 1M+ monthly users, with better Core Web Vitals, maintainability, and fewer production defects before release.

AI-Assisted Product Development

Trigal Media · MVPs across legal, healthcare, education, and SMB

Senior Web Developer Lead · Freelance / Part-time · 2025–Current

Problem

A startup needed practical MVPs with AI capabilities, a junior-heavy team, and tight delivery cycles — without turning every product into a one-off prototype.

What I built

Architecture direction, code reviews, and hands-on delivery. Integrated AI APIs and RAG workflows where the product required them, and set reusable patterns so the team could ship maintainable foundations.

Architecture

React, Next.js, and Astro on the client; WordPress and Laravel where they fit the product; TypeScript across the stack. AI APIs and RAG layered onto those foundations rather than replacing them.

Decision

Treat AI as a delivery multiplier — prompt engineering, custom rules, and reusable skills in Cursor — while architecture, code review, and product judgment stay human-owned.

Result

Faster MVP delivery across several verticals, more consistent engineering through mentoring and standards, and AI-enabled products that remain maintainable after the first ship.
